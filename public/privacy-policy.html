1.Who this policy covers

This policy applies to everyone who interacts with TruDev, including:

  • Employers who create an account to build and send assessments to candidates.
  • Candidates who are invited to take an assessment, whether or not they create an account.
  • Visitors to trudev.io who are not registered users.

3.How we use information

We use personal data to:

  • Create and maintain employer and candidate accounts
  • Deliver, score, and display technical assessments
  • Send transactional emails — assessment invitations, results notifications, account verification, password resets, and status updates
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Monitor, secure, and improve the reliability of the platform
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Comply with legal obligations
We do not sell personal data, and we do not use candidate assessment data to train third-party advertising models.

4.Email communications

All email TruDev sends is transactional and triggered by an action taken on the platform — for example, an employer inviting a candidate, or a candidate submitting an assessment. We do not send bulk marketing or promotional email, and we do not purchase, rent, or scrape email lists.

Every email we send:

  • Is triggered by a specific, identifiable action tied to an active account or assessment
  • Comes from a domain authenticated with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC
  • Is monitored for bounces and complaints, which are automatically suppressed from future sends

Because our email is transactional rather than promotional, there is no general marketing list to unsubscribe from. Registered users can manage which non-essential notifications they receive from their account settings at any time.

5.Legal basis for processing

Where required (for example, under the GDPR), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide the assessment platform to employers and candidates
  • Legitimate interests: securing our platform, preventing fraud, and improving our services
  • Consent: where you have given it, such as for optional communications
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose data to comply with the law

6.How we share information

We share personal data only in these circumstances:

  • With employers: a candidate's assessment results and submission are shared with the employer who invited them.
  • Service providers: vetted vendors who help us operate the platform — including hosting, email delivery (Amazon SES), analytics, and payment processing — under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TruDev, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy or a comparable one.

7.Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed to provide our services and for legitimate business or legal purposes. Account data is retained while an account is active. Candidate assessment data is retained for the period agreed with the inviting employer, after which it is deleted or anonymized unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8.Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular review of our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9.Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

10.Cookies and tracking

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences, and limited analytics cookies to understand how the platform is used and to improve it. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

11.Children's privacy

TruDev is intended for use by working professionals and job candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

12.International data transfers

TruDev may process and store data in countries other than your own. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we use appropriate safeguards required by applicable data protection law.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify registered users directly.
